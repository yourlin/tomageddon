## Boss

Appear on the last wave of a chapter and enter phase two at half HP. The wave lasts 90 seconds; after that the boss enrages: every 10 seconds its damage ×1.25 and one stack of enrage pressure is added (the player loses 3% Max HP × 1.25^stacks per second, ignoring dodge, armor and invulnerability, uncapped), so the fight always ends.

## Elite Affixes

Elites roll random affixes; from wave 7, regular monsters may spawn as affixed champions (HP ×3.5, drops ×4, guaranteed crate).

| Affix | Effect |
| --- | --- |
| Swift | Move Speed +35% |
| Armored | Damage taken -30%, immune to knockback |
| Berserk | Enrage when HP is below 40% |
| Regenerating | Regenerates 1.5% HP per second |
| Frost | Attacks inflict Slow |
| Venomous | Attacks inflict 3 stacks of Poison |
| Cursed | Attacks inflict Curse (no healing) |
| Shielded | Gains Barrier for 3s every 8s |
| Explosive | Explodes on death |
| Vampiric | Heals when dealing damage |
| Thorny | Melee hits reflect 5% of damage (reduced by armor; at most 2% Max HP per hit, 8% per second) |
| Commander | Nearby monsters gain Haste |
| Giant | +60% HP and a bigger body |
| Brutal | +40% damage |
| Searing | Attacks inflict 2 stacks of Burn |
| Rending | Attacks inflict 2 stacks of Bleed |
| Enfeebling | Attacks inflict Weaken |
| Unstoppable | Immune to slow, stun and knockback |
| Wealthy | Drops 3× Seeds |
| Splitting | Splits into 2 minions of the same kind on death |
