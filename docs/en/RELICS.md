Relics are new in 1.4.0: each one bends the rules instead of just adding stats. 45 relics and 8 sets. Sources: a pick-of-3 after the wave 5 and wave 10 elites, one every 10 waves in Endless, and occasionally from the Mysterious Merchant. Collecting 3 relics of the same set triggers a bonus. All effect text is generated from data.
