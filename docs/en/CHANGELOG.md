## v0.7.2 · 2026-10-17

**Chapter 6 gets harder: a tougher Blight Gardener and new late-chapter enemies, while Rot Vine is toned down**


- Chapter 6 boss Blight Gardener has about 67% more HP and 11% more damage, and enters its Overgrowth phase at 45% HP instead of 35%
- Four more enemy types now appear in the second half of Chapter 6
- Rot Vine deals less damage, charges less often and inflicts weaker Bleed
- Enemy HP raised in Chapters 6 and 7

## v0.7.1 · 2026-10-16

**Aura buffs, recipe-item restock rework, Chapter 4 difficulty spike fixed, rage super weapon freeze fixed**


- Fixed Dragon Cleaver and Hell Trident rage bursts chaining into each other endlessly, which caused stutters and errors
- Caramel Aura, Honey Aura, Rice Cooker Aura, Charcoal Aura, Blender, Sea Salt Ward and Taro Aura deal 50% more damage; weapons crafted from them keep their previous strength
- Shop restock of missing recipe items: chance lowered from 25% to 10%, only offered once your Luck can already roll that rarity (Epic needs 15, Legendary 20), and only until you own your first T4 weapon
- Kiwi Detective now starts with the Mustard Flamer and gets +2 Elemental instead of +1 Melee; the Star Anise Shuriken can still be picked at the start
- Chapter 4 wave 5 no longer introduces five new enemy types alongside its first elite; some now appear in waves 6 and 7
- Enemy HP raised in Chapters 2–7

## v0.7.0 · 2026-10-16

**Multi-phase bosses, themed arenas per chapter, double-synergy weapons, reworked weapon sets, 19 new weapons and 22 new super weapons**


- Every boss has 2–4 phases: at each HP threshold it plays a transition (freeze, charge, burst, camera move), gains new attacks and a new look, and the arena changes; each phase lasts at least 6s, so every boss survives at least 20s
- The Rot King's 3rd phase summons shadows of earlier bosses to fight alongside it
- The Cockroach Emperor is invulnerable while charging
- Each chapter has its own arena shape and size: frying pan, garden, fridge, cross-shaped junkyard, factory, octagonal garden, rot nest; obstacles block movement only, not bullets
- Every character now has 2 synergy tags: a weapon with either tag is a synergy weapon (+10% damage, synergy effect); with both it is a double-synergy weapon and also gains a synergy skill plus two tag boosts
- 7 new weapon tags (Polearm, Martial, Seed, Mech, Arcane, Bow, Thrown); all 39 characters have a unique tag pair and start with one of their double-synergy weapons by default
- 19 new character-themed weapons and 22 new character-themed super weapons
- Crafted weapons inherit their materials’ tags: same-name merges keep them, two different weapons combine both sets of tags
- Weapon sets reworked into 23 sets of specific weapons (counting distinct members; super weapons belong to no set); active tiers show in green and inactive ones in gray in weapon details
- Clearer crafting: every ready recipe gets its own "Craft into X" button, plus "View recipes"; shop items that are exactly a missing recipe material get a 🔨 badge
- Character balance: Chili, Tomato, Pea, Soybean, Peach and others buffed; Taro, Mushroom, Watermelon, Durian, Ginger and others nerfed; Soybean now summons 8 bean soldiers, one in each direction
- Enemy HP raised in Chapters 2–7; fewer weak minions in Chapter 3

## v0.6.6 · 2026-10-14

**Slower enemy bullets, and crowds of ranged enemies fire less often**


- Enemy volleys slow down from 2 shots on (2 shots ×0.85, 3 shots ×0.77, 9+ shots ×0.6); regular ranged minions’ bullets are a further ×0.9
- With more than 4 regular ranged minions on the field, each fires less often as their numbers grow (up to ×2.5 interval); elites and bosses are unaffected
- Chapter 5 enemy HP +10%, Chapter 4 −5% for a smoother difficulty curve

## v0.6.5 · 2026-10-13

**Big balance pass: weapon types get their own strengths, damage over time scales with Elemental damage, weak characters buffed, iPhone low-memory mode**


- Bullets gain type traits: snipers pierce 5 and deal +30% to enemies at ≥ 50% HP; shotguns pierce 2–3 and deal +25% up close; heavy single shots pierce 1–2 with stronger knockback and a chance to stagger; pistols / machine guns stack Suppression (slow)
- Melee thrusts: +15% damage, +15% crit chance, and the hitbox widens from a line to a narrow cone
- Burn, Poison, Bleed and other damage over time scale with Elemental damage (+15% per point); 8 DoT characters each gain a unique perk (stacking, stack cap, damage, duration, vulnerability, slow, crits, spreading)
- Chain lightning falls off faster per jump (0.85 → 0.75); Ember Mine, Mixer Storm, Spore Cannon and Glacier Mortar deal −25% damage
- Character changes: Tomato, Pea Soldier, Corn Gunner, Lychee, Cherry, Peach, Asparagus, Strawberry, Sprout and others buffed (new traits or stats); Mushroom and Taro slightly nerfed
- Sprout’s Growth Spurt now grants +30% XP from kills for 5s
- Enemy volleys: the more bullets, the slower they fly (down to ×0.6); 2- and 3-shot volleys spread wider so side shots miss you beyond half range
- Chapter 7 shortened to 25 waves with reworked spawns and terrain; ranged weapons orbit farther out while melee and flame weapons stay close
- New affix Short Fuse (halved self-destruct fuse, only on bursting / exploding foes); weapons redrawn with unique designs; character details scroll
- iPhone (Safari / WeChat) uses a low-memory mode to reduce freezes or WeChat restarts while loading

## v0.6.0 · 2026-10-08

**"The Crafting Road": weapons match characters by tag, 60 new fusion weapons, a crafting table and storage — craft T3 → T4 → super weapons; sharper visuals and 4 skins per character**


- Character synergy now works by weapon tag: any weapon with a synergy tag counts (+10% damage and triggers the talent)
- 60 new fusion weapons (199 total): every T3 has at least 2 recipes to T4, every T4 has a unique recipe; super weapons are crafted from two specific T4s
- Recipes need specific items: T4s need specific Epic items, super weapons the catalyst + 1 specific Legendary; the shop restocks missing items when you are close
- Crafting table: category filters, a recommended view (synergy first) and a horizontal upgrade tree; tap a weapon to see its recipes and what is missing
- 6-slot storage: weapons bought with a full bar go to storage; swap, sell or use them as recipe materials
- The shop never sells T4s; buying it out counts as a reroll; prices use the seed icon; stats show in two columns with icons
- HiDPI rendering: sharp on high-density screens and the desktop build (toggle in Settings); the desktop build starts fullscreen
- Mobile: larger touch targets; the shop, weapon popup and crafting table are scaled up
- Redesigned pause screen: character and equipped weapons on the left (hover or tap for stats), two-column stats on the right
- Character "traits" and "stat modifiers" merged into one "Stats & traits" list generated from the real values
- Character unlocks are paced out: usually 0–2 per run as you progress; unlock toasts and the results screen show the character
- 4 skins per character (120 / 160 / 200 / 250 Golden Tomatoes; the first is free at Mastery 10), cosmetic only
- 81 new achievements: fusion weapons, the crafting table, challenge modifiers, the talent tree and Master talents; "Fully Legendary" now also counts in Endless after wave 15
- Titles: categories, rarity, search and glowing title badges; the share poster shows more stats and your build
- Negative stats show in red; damage / heal numbers are bigger and last longer; damage-over-time numbers are colored by status (Burn red, Poison green…); fixed tapping "Combine" sometimes selling the weapon

- Chapter 6 "Rotting Greenhouse", hidden Chapter 7 "Rot Garden" and a true final boss
- 12 new weapons, 8 evolutions, 30 new items and 23 item combos
- Event waves, risky routes, the Mysterious Merchant, in-wave mini-quests, weather and 5 new terrain hazards — introduced chapter by chapter from chapter 2
- Longer chapters from chapter 5: 20 waves, then +5 per chapter (up to 50), with an elite every 5 waves

## v0.4.0 · 2026-10-01

**Weapon evolution, daily / weekly challenges, run stats and history — plus tips for new players**


- Weapon evolution: a T4 weapon plus a specific item evolves in the shop into one of 12 super weapons, keeping affixes and forge level; the shop shows recipes
- Daily / weekly challenges: a fixed character, chapter, ruleset and shop every day and every week, 15 rule modifiers; personal bests and streaks are tracked
- Run stats: damage by source (each weapon, skill, damage over time…) and seeds per wave; new "History" on the main menu keeps your last 30 runs
- Tutorial tips the first time you meet movement, the shop, combining, affixes, evolution, elites, bosses, talents and more — re-enable them in Settings
- Talent balance: Shadow Dance invulnerability cut to 0.35s, Unyielding now gets you up once per run at 25% HP, Battle Lust caps at 3 stacks, Execution at 8%, and some HP / attack speed / pickup talents reduced
- New boss music: an intense 168 BPM track that kicks in when the boss appears and hands back to the chapter music once it falls
- Healing balance: far fewer fruit drops with a per-wave cap, collected by pickup range; life steal triggers at most every 0.25s, at 40% rate for area damage, capped at 30%; healing skills heal about half as much with longer cooldowns; HP Regen is now clearly per 5 seconds
- The stat panel shows the Dodge (60%) and Life Steal (30%) caps
- 18 new achievements (evolution, challenges)

**New talent tree and Endless mode, plus 847 achievements**

## v0.2.0 · 2026-09-30

**Full ultimate animations, 50 new monsters, 32 new weapons, and a reworked economy**


- Skill animations: casting now shows the skill name, rotating light rays and a camera punch, with a distinct effect per skill form — shockwaves, light pillars, speed lines, screen-wide lightning, summoning circles
- 50 new monsters (77 total), 15 per chapter with a distinct roster each
- 32 new weapons (50 total): 17 melee, 18 ranged, 15 elemental
- Favored weapons: most characters have 3 weapons matching their talent for +20% damage, and they show up more often in the shop

- Level-ups only offer damage options for the weapon classes you actually own

- High-tier weapon rates recalibrated: T4 appears from wave 7 onward and is more common in later chapters
- Chapter difficulty recalibrated with smoother HP and damage curves for chapters 2–5
