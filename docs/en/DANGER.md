Tomato Danger is the post-clear difficulty ladder with 20 levels: clearing level N of a chapter unlocks level N+1 for that chapter, and each level stacks one more rule on top of all previous ones. Higher Danger gives more talent points, seeds and Golden Tomatoes after the run. Bosses gain extra moves at Danger 10 / 15 / 20; a character who clears Danger 20 in any chapter gets a gold portrait frame.
