# AI 生成内容声明 / AI-Generated Content Disclosure

## 中文

**可爱风**画风下的美术为 AI 生成（经人工挑选与后期处理），包括：

- 角色、怪物、Boss 的形象与动画帧
- 章节地图（地面、前景、地图物体）与关卡缩略图
- 武器与弹体插画
- 主菜单封面、开场四格漫画插画

**复古风**画风完全由程序绘制，不使用上述任何 AI 生成的贴图。

游戏的玩法、数值、关卡设计与代码由作者编写；开发中使用 AI 编程助手仅作为效率工具。游戏**不会**在运行时实时生成 AI 内容。

## English

The **Cute** art style uses AI-generated artwork (curated and post-processed by hand), including:

- Character, monster and boss artwork and animation frames
- Chapter maps (ground, foreground, map objects) and stage thumbnails
- Weapon and projectile illustrations
- The main-menu cover and the opening comic illustrations

The **Retro** art style is drawn entirely by code and uses none of the AI-generated textures above.

Gameplay, balance, level design and code are written by the author; AI coding assistants were used only as productivity tools during development. The game does **not** generate any AI content at runtime.
